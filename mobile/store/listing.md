# Google Play store listing: eCampus MMU

**App name** (max 30): eCampus MMU

**Short description** (max 80):
Timetables, attendance and lecture reminders for your university.

**Full description** (max 4000):

eCampus MMU brings teaching and learning management for your university into one app, for students, lecturers, class representatives, heads of department, deans and administrators.

FOR STUDENTS
• See your weekly lecture timetable with rooms and lecturers
• Get reminders before upcoming lectures
• View your attendance history for every course unit

FOR CLASS REPRESENTATIVES
• Mark who is present during a lecture in a few taps
• Send the register to the lecturer for approval

FOR LECTURERS
• View your teaching times and rooms
• Approve or return attendance registers from your class reps
• Get reminders for your upcoming lectures

FOR HEADS OF DEPARTMENT, DEANS AND ADMINISTRATORS
• Set up faculties, departments, programmes, course units, classes and rooms
• Allocate teaching load with cross-cutting units first, and get warnings when they are missing
• Generate the timetable with automatic checks for room, lecturer and class clashes
• See attendance and teaching reports for your unit

Sign in with the account your university gives you. A new institution can create its university from the app with its own name, logo, contacts and theme colour.

A demo university is available on the default server so you can try every role.
