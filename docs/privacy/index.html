eCampus

Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

eCampus ("the app") helps universities manage teaching and learning: timetables, lecture allocation, attendance and lecture reminders. Each university that uses the app controls its own data. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

Information we collect

  • Account details your university provides: name, title, position, email address, staff or registration number, role, faculty, department and class.
  • Contact details you choose to add in your profile: phone numbers, a contact email, office, address and an emergency contact, and an optional profile photo.
  • Academic records created while using the app: timetables, teaching allocations, attendance registers (who was present at which lecture) and their approval status.
  • Preferences: lecture reminder settings and in-app notifications.
  • University details entered by administrators, including an uploaded university logo image.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or payment information, it does not access your photo library except for an image you choose to upload, and it contains no advertising.

How information is used

Only to provide the service to your university: signing you in, showing your timetable, recording and approving attendance, producing reports for authorised staff (administrators, Deans and Heads of Department within their unit) and sending lecture reminders.

Sharing

Your information is visible only to authorised members of your own university according to their role. We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for marketing. Data may be disclosed if required by law.

Security and storage

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Data is kept while your university uses the service.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules lecture reminders on your device. You can turn reminders off at any time under Alerts.

Deleting your account

You can ask to delete your account in the app (More → Profile & password → Delete my account) or on the web. Your university's systems administrator approves the request; the account is then archived (you can no longer sign in) for the period your university sets, normally 30 days, and your personal details are erased after that. See how to delete your account. Anonymised attendance statistics may be kept by your university.

Children

The app is intended for university staff and students and is not directed at children under 13.
